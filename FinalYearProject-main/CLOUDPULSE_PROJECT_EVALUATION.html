9. Research Problem

What problem does the paper solve?
In large-scale cloud systems (Microservices, DBaaS, Serverless), incoming workload rates change non-linearly with sudden spikes and concept drift. Standard models output single-point estimates (e.g. "Load = 800 req/s") which lack uncertainty quantification. The paper solves the Multi-step Online Time Series Forecasting (MOTSF) problem by constructing adaptive, uncertainty-aware prediction intervals.

Why is this problem important?
Reactive auto-scaling (e.g., standard Kubernetes HPA) triggers after a server is already overloaded. Because container cold-starts and initialization take 30 to 90 seconds, reactive systems suffer severe tail-latency spikes and SLA breaches. Predictive scaling provisions capacity before the traffic surge arrives.

10. Existing Method (IPOC Framework)

The paper introduces IPOC (Interval Prediction with Online Chasing):

  • Adaptive Copula Conformal Inference (ACCI): Transforms point forecasts into distribution-free confidence intervals $[L_{t+h}, U_{t+h}]$ using copula functions to model cross-step error correlations.
  • Chasing Oracle: An online learning optimizer that updates model weights on streaming telemetry without requiring full offline retraining.
  • Datasets Evaluated: 5 real-world production datasets including ByteDance Cloud production traces, Alibaba Cluster Trace, and Google Cluster Data.
  • Results: IPOC achieved an average reduction of >20% in RMSE, MAE, SMAPE, and ρ-risk against 25 baselines (ARIMA, Informer, DeepAR, PatchTST).

11. Research Gap

  • Gaps from Authors: The paper focuses on offline/simulated trace validation rather than deploying a live, closed-loop Kubernetes controller.
  • Additional Gaps Identified:
    1. No Reactive Fail-Safe: If an unforeseen traffic anomaly occurs outside predicted intervals, pure predictive scaling can fail without a hybrid arbitration layer.
    2. No Joint Cost-SLA Optimization: Intervals are not mapped to dollar costs vs. SLA violation penalty matrices.
    3. No Real-Time Visualization: Lacks an interactive DevOps control dashboard.

15. Proposed Novelties (5 Technical Contributions)

  1. Asymmetric Conformal Risk Scaling: Penalizes under-prediction 3× more heavily than over-prediction to guarantee 0% SLA drops.
  2. Hybrid Dual-Loop Arbiter: Blends proactive predictive scaling (60s in advance) with reactive fail-safe thresholds.
  3. Multi-Objective SLA vs. Dollar Cost Optimizer: Formulates replica sizing as a Pareto optimization problem.
  4. Online Concept Drift Detection: Auto-triggers incremental model fine-tuning when residual error exceeds drift bounds.
  5. Carbon-Intensity Aware Modulation: Throttles non-critical background jobs during regional peak carbon-intensity hours.
